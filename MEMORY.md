# Color Me Lucky Memory

- The host is **Susie Adriance**.
- Registered players are **Stephanny, Giann, Francisco, Lyka, and Jenny**.
- Players select their registered name without a password; host controls require authentication and role authorization.
- A player’s 60-second round begins with their own randomized 13-question primary pool. After Q13, their five-question reserve pool continues automatically without question reuse.
- The supplied 25-question reserve bank is **not** a tiebreaker bank.
- Tiebreakers use five dedicated questions from the original specification. The winner is the first eligible player with a correct answer received by the server; if all tied players answer incorrectly, the next tiebreaker question is used; after five unanswered tiebreakers, the tie stands and prizes split.
- The correct answer to the team question about Roo’s breed is **Shorkie Poo**.
- The visual direction is “Studio Signal”: deep navy, gold broadcast cues, red/blue/green/yellow answer controls, high contrast text, and a game-show stage.
