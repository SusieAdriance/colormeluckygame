# Chosen Direction: Studio Signal

## Design Movement

**Broadcast modernism** meets the measured drama of a televised game-show production board. The page should feel prepared, intentional, and shareable rather than like a generic landing page or a dense technical document.

## Core Principles

1. **On-air hierarchy:** The essential verdict and readiness signals are immediately visible, with detailed findings staged below like a producer’s rundown.
2. **Purposeful color:** Red, blue, green, and yellow appear as functional signal marks and answer-color references, never as incidental decoration.
3. **Controlled spectacle:** Gold highlights, light beams, and slightly kinetic edges convey game-show energy without overwhelming practical content.
4. **Credible clarity:** Every decorative flourish must support scanning, ownership, or understanding of the technical review.

## Color Philosophy

The visual field begins in deep midnight navy to echo the original game concept and allow gold to read as a premium broadcast signal. An off-white document surface inside the page makes the review calm to read, while the four owned answer colors serve as immediate status labels. The signature gold carries “ready for air” confidence rather than luxury for its own sake.

## Layout Paradigm

The page is a **vertical production rundown**, not a conventional centered marketing funnel. A persistent desktop rail lists the named review acts. The main content alternates between wide editorial panels, offset facts, a dark compute stage, and a horizontal decision strip. Each section receives a numbered cue and an individual visual rhythm.

## Signature Elements

1. A four-color **signal wheel** mark with a gold center, used in the header and as a recurring visual cue.
2. A **live readiness meter** that turns the executive conclusion into a visible signal rather than a paragraph alone.
3. Broadcast-style **cue labels** such as “01 / Capacity” and “Decision required,” paired with narrow rules and sharp corner details.

## Interaction Philosophy

Navigation should behave like moving through a run-of-show: crisp anchor jumps, active section states, and expanded decision cards on demand. Hover states brighten signal colors and make individual facts feel selected by a producer, while all content remains usable by keyboard.

## Animation

On first load, the hero copy enters in short staggered motions and the readiness meter settles into place. Decorative rays drift slowly and independently; cards lift only a few pixels on hover. Transitions use 160–240 ms custom ease-out timing and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, where all nonessential motion is removed.

## Typography System

**Bebas Neue** provides compressed, uppercase broadcast headlines and cue numbers. **DM Sans** provides generous, highly legible review copy. **IBM Plex Mono** is reserved for capacity metrics, event labels, and technical state. Headlines are sharp and oversized; body copy uses long line lengths only inside limited-width reading panels.

## Brand Essence

**Color Me Lucky is a hosted, live team trivia ritual that turns a short video-call break into a high-energy shared game show.**

Personality adjectives: **electric, considered, inclusive.**

## Brand Voice

Headlines should sound decisive and stage-ready. CTAs and microcopy should feel like a show producer giving a clear cue, never like generic corporate web copy.

Example lines: “**Six browsers. One live room. Ready for air.**”  
“**Resolve these four calls before the countdown starts.**”

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a bold, text-free four-segment signal wheel: red, blue, green, and yellow quadrants turn around a gold star-like center. It looks like both a game-show buzzer and a compact broadcast source indicator. The wordmark is rendered with extended, condensed display type rather than a default font.

## Signature Brand Color

**Signal Gold — #E2B714.**

## Style Decisions

- The site uses **Studio Signal** consistently; avoid purple gradients, generic card grids, and soft consumer-app styling.
- Dark visual moments must use light text and an overlay where an image is present; light document panels always use deep navy text.
- The four answer colors are never the only carrier of meaning; every status also includes a label, icon, or text cue.
